# Mesa — Landing Page Draft

## Hero
**Your tabs, in space.**
Mesa turns your browser into an infinite canvas. Spread tabs out, zoom out to see the whole session, zoom in to do the work. Click a tab and it goes live over a blurred map of everything else — so you never lose context.

*A Firefox extension for people whose tabs outgrew the strip.*

## The problem
Tabs live in a one-inch strip across the top. After twenty of them you can't read the titles, can't find the one you wanted, can't see the shape of what you're doing. You open a window for "research," another for "the project," another for "that thing later" — and still lose things. You have spatial memory. The tab strip doesn't use it.

## The solution
Mesa gives your tabs a place — a 2D canvas you pan and zoom, like Figma or a whiteboard. Each tab is a card. Arrange them how you think. Group a research session into a region. Zoom out and see the whole shape of your day. Zoom in and work.

## How it works — 3 steps
1. **See the map.** Hit a hotkey. Your tabs appear as cards on an infinite canvas.
2. **Go live.** Click a card. That tab pops up live, filling the screen — with the rest of your canvas blurred behind it, still there, still in context.
3. **Back to the map.** Hit Escape. The live tab steps back into its card. Pan to the next thing.

## Features
- **Infinite pan & zoom** — your whole session on one surface.
- **Snapshot cards** — every tab, thumbnailed, even the background ones.
- **Pop-to-live** — click a card, it's the real tab, fully interactive.
- **Blurred-focus** — the map stays behind you, defocused, so context never vanishes.
- **Spatial groups** — box a cluster, label it, collapse it.
- **Auto-links** — see which tabs opened from which.
- **Persists** — your layout survives restarts. Reopen a whole region in one click.

## Pitch line
Stop hunting through a strip. Spread your tabs on a canvas.

## FAQ
- **Is it live or screenshots?** Both. The map is snapshots; the moment you click, it's the live tab.
- **Does it replace my tabs?** No. Your tabs are real tabs the whole time. Mesa is the map over them.
- **Firefox only?** Yes, for now — it relies on Firefox's ability to thumbnail any tab, even background ones.
- **Will it eat memory?** Cards are small images. Only the card you pop is live.
